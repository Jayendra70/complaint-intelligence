**## Headline**  
Wells Fargo sees sharp spikes in mortgage‑modification denials, unauthorized account actions, and auto‑loan disputes during the week of 2023‑01‑16.  

**## Trend**  
- Mortgage‑modification complaints rose sharply, with five distinct cases reporting denied or stalled modifications and poor communication [ID: 6466819][ID: 6455878][ID: 6469081][ID: 6471626][ID: 6453491].  
- Account‑related complaints surged, dominated by unauthorized freezes, fraud‑related calls, and unexplained closures [ID: 6447304][ID: 6469648][ID: 6472262][ID: 6466654][ID: 6447374].  
- Auto‑loan issues increased, featuring loan‑term misunderstandings, recall‑related service problems, and negative‑equity surprises [ID: 6471329][ID: 6449515][ID: 6466533][ID: 6459025][ID: 6449754].  

**## Evidence**  
- **Mortgage modifications:** Customers reported being refused HAMP and VA modifications despite prior assurances, repeated document requests, and loan sales without notice, leading to foreclosures and trial‑payment disputes [ID: 6466819][ID: 6455878][ID: 6469081][ID: 6471626][ID: 6453491].  
- **Account actions:** Callers received fraudulent “FBI” warnings, had checking and savings accounts blocked without notice, experienced permanent PayPal‑style holds, and saw multiple unauthorized card charges that were not flagged by fraud systems [ID: 6447304][ID: 6469648][ID: 6472262][ID: 6466654][ID: 6447374].  
- **Vehicle loans:** Borrowers described confusion over loan contracts, a vehicle recall that left the loan unchanged, a $24 K negative‑equity position shortly after purchase, and credit‑reporting errors that listed loans as outstanding after payoff [ID: 6471329][ID: 6449515][ID: 6466533][ID: 6459025][ID: 6449754].  

**## Recommended action**  
1. **Mortgage team:** Conduct an audit of modification eligibility criteria, streamline document intake, and establish a clear escalation path for denied cases to reduce repeat filings.  
2. **Fraud & account services:** Deploy real‑time fraud alerts, require multi‑factor verification before account freezes, and create a rapid‑response unit for unauthorized‑access complaints.  
3. **Auto‑loan division:** Review loan disclosures for clarity, implement a recall‑impact protocol that adjusts loan terms when vehicles are serviced, and reconcile credit‑reporting data promptly after payoff.  

These steps should curb the current spikes and improve customer trust.
